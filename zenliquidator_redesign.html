Common Questions

FAQ

What is a manifested truckload?
A manifested load comes with an item-level spreadsheet: UPC/SKU, description, original retail (MSRP), quantity, and condition. This lets you calculate margin before you commit. Unmanifested loads have no list and ship at a steeper discount — buyer takes more risk for more upside.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We export 40' containers (FCL) and consolidated pallets to 40+ countries, primarily LATAM, the Caribbean, West Africa, and Southeast Asia. We handle BOL, commercial invoice, packing list, and basic HS classification. Buyer is responsible for destination customs and duties.
What payment methods do you accept?
Wire transfer (preferred for orders over $5k), ACH, credit card (3% processing fee), and Net-30 terms for approved repeat buyers after 3 successful orders.
Can I inspect inventory before buying?
Absolutely. Walk-ins welcome at our Lawrenceville, GA facility Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm ET. We recommend booking a slot in advance so a rep can pull the specific lots you're considering.
What's the difference between customer returns, overstock, and shelf pulls?
Customer returns: items returned by end consumers (mixed condition). Overstock: brand-new, unsold inventory in original packaging. Shelf pulls: items removed from store shelves to make room for new seasonal stock — typically new, may have minor packaging wear.
Is there a minimum order?
No hard minimum. We sell anything from a single case (~$200) to multi-container exports. Per-unit pricing improves significantly at the pallet and truckload tier.
